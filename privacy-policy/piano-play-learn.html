Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use Piano Play: Learn Piano (the "App").

This Privacy Policy also explains your privacy rights and choices regarding the information that may be collected when you use the App.

By using the App, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Collection and Use

Piano Play: Learn Piano is designed to provide piano learning and practice functionality without requiring users to create an account or register.

You can use the core functionality of the App without providing us with personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, password, or postal address.

We do not intentionally collect personally identifiable information through account registration because the App does not require user registration for its core functionality.

Learning and Practice Information

The App may store information related to your piano learning and practice activity in order to provide progress tracking and other App features.

This information may include:

  • Lesson completion status
  • Daily practice duration
  • Learning streak information
  • Practice statistics
  • Learning progress
  • App preferences and settings

This information is primarily stored locally on your device and is used to provide the learning and progress-tracking functionality of the App.

2. Local Storage

Piano Play: Learn Piano uses local storage on your device to save certain App data, including learning progress, lesson completion, practice statistics, streak information, and preferences.

We do not require an online account or registration to save this information.

Local data may be removed when the App is deleted from your device, depending on your device settings, backups, and Apple's operating system behavior.

3. Piano Audio and SoundFont Files

Piano Play: Learn Piano provides a virtual piano keyboard with piano sounds generated using embedded SoundFont (SF2) files.

Piano audio is generated locally within the App. The App does not require streaming copyrighted music in order to provide its piano-playing functionality.

The App does not upload your piano playing or locally generated piano audio to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide advertising, purchase processing, diagnostics, and other functionality.

These third-party services may collect certain information from your device according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services that may be used by the App include:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob
  • Apple Privacy Policy

5. Advertising

The free version of Piano Play: Learn Piano may display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect certain information from your device for purposes such as advertising, advertising measurement, fraud prevention, and providing relevant advertisements.

The information collected by advertising providers may include device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location information, and information about interaction with advertisements, depending on your device settings and applicable permissions.

For more information about Google's advertising practices, please visit:

Google Advertising Policies and Technologies

6. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS, the App may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework where required.

If permission is requested, it may be used to allow personalized advertising through Google AdMob.

You can choose to allow or deny tracking permission.

If you deny tracking permission, you can continue using the App and its core piano learning and practice features without limitations caused by that decision.

You can change your tracking preferences at any time through your iOS device settings.

7. Premium Purchase

Piano Play: Learn Piano may offer an optional Premium purchase.

Premium may provide benefits such as:

  • Ad-free experience
  • Access to all available lessons
  • Uninterrupted learning and practice
  • Additional Premium features that may be introduced in the future

Premium is optional. Users can continue using the free version of the App with its applicable features and advertising.

8. Payments and App Store Purchases

Any Premium purchase made through the iOS App is processed through Apple's App Store and Apple's payment systems.

We do not collect or store your complete credit card number, debit card number, bank account information, or other complete payment credentials on our servers.

Apple may provide information necessary to verify your purchase entitlement and provide access to purchased features.

Apple's handling of payment and account information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

For more information, please review:

Apple Privacy Policy

9. Log Data and Diagnostics

In the event of an error, crash, or technical issue, diagnostic information may be collected through Apple or third-party services used by the App.

Depending on the service and your device settings, diagnostic information may include:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Crash information
  • Error information
  • Date and time of use
  • General technical information

This information may be used to identify errors, improve App stability, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve the overall user experience.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The core functionality of Piano Play: Learn Piano does not intentionally use browser cookies.

However, third-party services integrated into the App, including advertising providers, may use device identifiers, SDK technologies, or similar technologies to provide their services.

The use of such technologies is governed by the respective third-party provider's privacy policy.

11. Data Security

We value your privacy and take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information.

12. Data Retention

Learning and practice information stored locally by the App remains on your device according to your device and App settings.

You may remove locally stored App information by deleting the App from your device, subject to Apple's backup and restoration behavior.

Information collected by third-party services is retained according to the respective third party's privacy policy and retention practices.

13. Links to Other Websites

The App or this Privacy Policy may contain links to third-party websites.

These websites are operated independently from us. We do not control and are not responsible for the content, privacy policies, or practices of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policy of any third-party website that you visit.

14. Children's Privacy

Piano Play: Learn Piano is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through account registration because the App does not require registration for its core functionality.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us. We will review the situation and take appropriate action where necessary.

15. Your Privacy Choices

You can control certain privacy and advertising preferences through your iOS device settings.

You may choose whether to grant App Tracking Transparency permission when requested.

You may also choose whether to purchase the optional Premium version of the App.

Declining tracking permission does not prevent you from using the core piano learning and practice features.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date displayed at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how information is handled.
